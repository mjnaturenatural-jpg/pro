# Managing products

Sign in at `/admin/login` with your administrator account. Never share your password or record it on screen.

## Update a price
1. Open Products and search for the product by name.
2. Select the product or its edit button. Under Pack Sizes & Pricing, change the MRP or selling price for the relevant pack.
3. Select Save Changes and check for the success message. Reopen the product to confirm the change persisted.
4. Open the product on the storefront and check the same pack size. The homepage can take up to a minute to refresh.

## Add a product
1. Open Products and select New Product. Enter the name, description and category.
2. Add at least one pack with a label, MRP, selling price and stock. Add more packs if needed.
3. Upload a JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF image (maximum 8 MB). Uploading requires the store's Cloudinary configuration.
4. Select Published when the product is ready, then select Create Product. Check the product list and storefront category.

## Edit details or images
1. Search for a product and open it. Change its description or category as needed.
2. Upload a new image, remove an individual image using its trash button, or select Set primary on an image.
3. Save and verify the product page. Published controls whether the product appears in the store; Featured affects its ordering in featured sections.

For problems signing in, uploading or saving, contact the site administrator. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens in support messages.
